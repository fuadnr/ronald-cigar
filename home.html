    OUR STORY

    Heritage,
    Flavor & Luxury

    Ronald Cigar is crafted for the gentleman who seeks a brief yet fulfilling escape amidst the demands of modern life. Each cigar tells a story of patience, precision, and passion.

    Produced by PT Cipta Gading Tembakao, we leverage decades of expertise in curating the finest tobacco leaves — establishing Ronald Cigar as the ultimate answer for aficionados seeking the perfect balance between heritage and modern luxury.

    Elegantly presented in our signature orange box, Ronald Cigar embodies sophistication and style suitable for any occasion — from the golf course to the boardroom.

    PRODUCTION PROCESS

    The Craftsmanship

    PT Cipta Gading Tembakao ensures that every Ronald Cigar undergoes a precise, time-honored journey from leaf to luxury.

    01
    SELECTION

    Only the finest tobacco leaves — selected for superior elasticity, texture, and aroma — make it past our first stage. Inferior leaves are rejected without compromise.

    02
    FERMENTATION

    A natural, chemical-free fermentation process that unlocks the authentic character of the tobacco — developing depth, complexity, and the signature Ronald Cigar aroma.

    03
    HAND-ROLLING

    Crafted by master rollers who ensure perfect density throughout. The result: a smooth yet firm draw that remains consistent from first light to final ash.

    04
    QUALITY CONTROL

    Each cigar is manually inspected before packaging. Only cigars that meet our exacting standards bear the Ronald Cigar name.
