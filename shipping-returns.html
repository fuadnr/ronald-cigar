    CUSTOMER CARE

    Shipping &
    Returns

    Last updated: November 2026  ·  PT Cipta Gading Tembakao

    01

    Where We Ship

    We deliver to addresses across Indonesia, from Sabang to Merauke, through our courier partners. You can also collect your order free of charge from our HQ in Tangerang.

    Delivery to remote areas may take longer than the estimates below. Same-day GoSend delivery is available only in Jabodetabek (DKI Jakarta and the surrounding areas of Jawa Barat and Banten).

    21+ Only. Every order is shipped only after payment is received and the buyer's age has been verified. The recipient must also be 21 or older.

    02

    Delivery Options & Costs

    The courier, delivery estimate, and shipping cost are shown at checkout before you pay. Estimates start from the day your order is handed to the courier.

    03

    Processing Times

    • Orders are processed within 2 business hours (Monday to Saturday, 09.00 to 17.00) once payment and age verification are complete.
    • Processed orders are handed to the courier by the next business day.
    • Orders placed on Sundays or public holidays are processed on the next business day.
    04

    Age Check on Delivery

    • Parcels are handed only to an adult aged 21 or older. The courier may ask the recipient to show a valid ID.
    • Parcels are not left with minors, with neighbours, or unattended.
    • If no eligible recipient is available, the courier may try again or return the parcel to us. We will contact you to arrange redelivery, and additional shipping costs may apply.
    05

    Packaging & Storage

    Every order is packed to protect the cigars from heat and humidity in transit, in our gift-ready box packaging.

    Once received, store your cigars in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Keep Mini Cigar Classic in its box, and keep Half Corona in a humidor if you do not plan to smoke it soon.

    06

    Tracking Your Order

    We send your tracking number by WhatsApp as soon as your parcel is handed to the courier. You can also check your order at any time on our Order Status page using your order number and phone number.

    07

    Pickup at HQ

    • Address: Ruko Maggiore Square 12A, Jl. Springs Boulevard No. 56, Kab. Tangerang, Banten 15332
    • Hours: Monday to Saturday, 09.00 to 17.00
    • Pickup orders are ready from the next business day after verification. We will message you on WhatsApp when your order is ready.
    • Please bring the ID used for age verification. Orders can only be collected by the buyer.
    08

    Returns & Damaged Items

    Because cigars are consumable and sensitive to storage conditions, we cannot accept returns of products that have been opened or that were delivered in good condition, including for a change of mind.

    If your order arrives damaged or incorrect
    • Contact us within 2 × 24 hours of delivery at support@ronaldcigar.co.id or on WhatsApp.
    • Include your order number, photos of the item and packaging, and an unboxing video.
    • After review, we will replace the affected items or refund their price, including the related shipping cost.
    09

    Cancellations

    You can cancel an unpaid order at any time on the payment page. Paid orders can be cancelled free of charge before they are dispatched. Once an order has been handed to the courier, it can no longer be cancelled.

    10

    Refunds

    Approved refunds are made to your original payment method within 7 business days. The time for funds to appear in your account depends on your bank or payment provider.
