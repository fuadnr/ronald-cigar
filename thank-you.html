COMMON QUESTIONS
When will I receive my tracking number?

We send your tracking number by WhatsApp as soon as your parcel is handed to the courier, usually within 1 business day after payment and age verification.

Can I change my delivery address?

Address changes are possible only before the parcel is handed to the courier. Contact us on WhatsApp right away with your order number.

Who can receive the parcel?

The recipient must be 21 or older and may be asked to show ID. The courier will not leave the parcel with minors or unattended.

What if my order arrives damaged?

Contact us within 2 × 24 hours of delivery with photos and an unboxing video. We will replace the item or refund it, as set out in our Terms of Service.
